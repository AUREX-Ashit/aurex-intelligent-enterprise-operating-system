## Context

The C-023 (Licensing & Entitlement) minimum Business Activity chartered as `WP-17 BA-01` ("Establish Entitlement/License Context (Administrative)") carries one unresolved `[D]` item — `WP-17 §8` (Persistence Target) and its mirror `TDS-C023 §24` item 1: **which microservice hosts C-023's Licensing/Entitlement governance-layer persistence** (`TDS-C023 §6.3`'s working-name `entitlement_license_registry`; canonically, `Master_Technical_Architecture.md`'s `license_registry` and `entitlement_registry` — see the explicit non-reconciliation note in Consequences). A formal R1 investigation established the evidence and options; the Repository Owner selected Option B-1 (AuthService, modular-monolith phase). This ADR records that decision.

The situation is directly analogous to the one `ADR-003` resolved for Organization Management (C-004):

- `CAP-001` and `ARCH-000` do not assign a microservice to any capability — that mapping is an implementation decision outside their registry schema, and `CLAUDE.md §8` requires each capability to have exactly one owning service. `Master_Technical_Architecture.md` likewise "explicitly does NOT specify … owning service" for its tables.
- No dedicated C-023 / Licensing / Commercial / Subscription / Billing service exists or is scaffolded anywhere. `Backend/Services/` contains `AIService`, `AuthService`, `IngestionService`, `ReportingService`, and `TenantService` only. `TenantService` remains a fully mocked scaffold, exactly as `ADR-003 §Decision(2)` recorded — not a candidate host and not a second owner of any capability.
- C-023's Primary Specification is `URA-001` (`CAP-001` line 77) — the same constitution that governs C-001/C-002/C-003/C-006/C-007, all hosted in `AuthService`. C-023's own domain-index grouping is `D-002` (Commercial & Subscription), whose other members (C-020/C-024/C-025) carry a different Primary Specification (`COM-001`, still Draft) and are Planned or Active-but-unbuilt; `COM-001` itself disclaims Entitlement and reaffirms C-023 as `URA-001`-owned.
- C-023's canonical data anchors are AuthService-owned: `license_registry.membership_id → membership_registry` (C-007) and `entitlement_registry.organization_id → organization_master` (C-004). `AuthService` implements these anchors as its own `memberships` / `organizations` tables.
- C-023's Decision-1 Commit Authority mechanism (`IRA-C023 §19.13`) — the `approval_authorities` registry (C-003, WP-02) plus the `membership_approval_authority` binding and the `require_approval_authority()` runtime dependency (C-003, WP-18, CLOSED — CERTIFIED — RELEASE-READY) — is entirely AuthService-owned.
- Every recent registry table has been placed in `AuthService` through its governing Technical Design: `tenant_registry` (WP-16 / `TDS-016`), `authority_holders` (`TDS-017`), `membership_approval_authority` (WP-18 / `TDS-018`).
- `TDS-C023 §14` (accepted, twice independently reviewed) specifies the Commit as a single-service, single-database-transaction flow modelled on `MembershipService.establish()` — validation → authority verification (fail-closed, before any write) → establishment inside one transaction → `record_audit()` → rollback on `IntegrityError`.

## Decision

1. **`AuthService` is the implementation owner for C-023 (Licensing & Entitlement) persistence and runtime operations during the current modular-monolith phase of the platform.** This covers the Licensing/Entitlement governance-layer registry (`TDS-C023 §6.3`; canonically `license_registry` / `entitlement_registry`), its repository, service, and any router/schema, and the runtime Commit Authority integration for `ERB-C023-05`.

2. **The canonical Organization and Membership anchors remain unchanged.** `membership_registry` and `organization_master` (implemented in `AuthService` as `memberships` and `organizations`) remain the canonical anchors for C-023's License and Entitlement records respectively, exactly as `Master_Technical_Architecture.md` and `PE-001-C023` specify.

3. **No cross-service foreign keys.** This decision SHALL NOT introduce, and SHALL NOT be used to justify, a service boundary that would require a cross-service database foreign-key relationship — prohibited by `CLAUDE.md §8` ("never access another service's database"). Because C-023's anchors and its Commit Authority mechanism are all AuthService-owned, co-location keeps every reference intra-service.

4. **No new service is created by this decision.** `CommercialService`, `LicensingService`, `SubscriptionService`, `BillingService`, or any other new service SHALL NOT be created, scaffolded, or reserved as part of this decision. Creating a new service boundary remains subject to `CLAUDE.md §18` / `§19.4` architectural-change control and is not authorized here.

5. **`TenantService` remains scaffolding** — not authoritative for Licensing/Entitlement data, not to be built out for this purpose, and not to be treated as a host or owner of any C-023 data, mirroring `ADR-003 §Decision(2)`.

6. **Future service extraction is a deferred consideration, not decided here.** The `D-002` direction — a dedicated Commercial/Subscription service (`CommercialService`) eventually hosting C-020, C-023, C-024, and C-025 together, or an extraction of C-023 into its own deployable service — is recorded as a legitimate future architectural direction to be **evaluated after the modular-monolith phase, as a separate, explicitly-scoped architectural decision** (a future ADR), not as a current implementation decision. This ADR neither authorizes nor forecloses it, mirroring `ADR-003 §Decision(3)`.

7. **Capability ownership is unchanged.** `AuthService` hosting does **not** transfer business-capability ownership away from C-023. **C-023 remains the owning business capability** for Licensing & Entitlement (`CAP-001`, `URA-001`), including the License governance layer assigned to it by `IRA-C023 §18.13` (Decision 6 — Split Ownership). `AuthService` is the current **implementation / service host** only. This ADR does not change `CAP-001`, `ARCH-000`, `URA-001`, or `COM-001` — it resolves an implementation-layer question those documents intentionally leave open.

8. **Scope of what this resolves.** This decision resolves the `WP-17 §8` (Persistence Target) `[D]` item and its mirror `TDS-C023 §24` item 1 — **and nothing else.** It does not resolve, narrow, or reinterpret any other `[D]` item, gate, or open question in `IRA-C023`, `TDS-C023`, or the `WP-17` charter.

## Rationale

Consistent with `ADR-003`'s own reasoning applied to an analogous situation: the only real, canonically-anchored home for C-023's persistence in the current modular-monolith phase is `AuthService`, because C-023's Primary Specification (`URA-001`), both of its canonical data anchors (`membership_registry`, `organization_master`), and its entire Decision-1 Commit Authority mechanism (`approval_authorities` + `membership_approval_authority` + `require_approval_authority()`) are already AuthService-owned. Hosting C-023 anywhere else would either require a cross-service foreign key (prohibited by `CLAUDE.md §8`) or force the polymorphic-reference indirection `TDS-C023 §6.3` designed, and would split the single-transaction Commit flow `TDS-C023 §14` specifies across a service boundary. Building or scaffolding a new service now would violate `CLAUDE.md §18` (no new service boundaries without approval) and, per `ADR-003`, is precisely the kind of extraction question that belongs to a later, separate decision once the modular-monolith phase ends and C-023's `D-002` neighbours (C-020, C-024, C-025) are themselves chartered and built. Consolidating implementation ownership in `AuthService` for the current phase is the lowest-risk, lowest-rework path and preserves "one capability, one owning service" (`CLAUDE.md §8`, Golden Rule 3) without a dedicated service.

## Consequences

- A future C-023 implementation (once separately and explicitly authorized — **not by this ADR**) builds its model, migration, repository, service, and any router/schema inside `Backend/Services/AuthService`, alongside the existing C-001/C-002/C-003/C-004/C-005/C-006/C-007 implementations and the `tenant_registry` (C-040) and `membership_approval_authority` (C-003) tables already hosted there.
- The C-023 records reference `membership` and `organization` by their AuthService-owned primary keys, intra-service — no cross-service foreign key, no polymorphic-reference indirection required for the co-located case. Whether `TDS-C023 §6.3`'s polymorphic-reference recommendation is simplified to real intra-service foreign keys is an implementation-design refinement for a future C-023 Technical Design / implementation session to record; it is a consequence permitted by this decision, not mandated by it, and it does not change `TDS-C023`'s accepted design intent.
- **The `license_registry` vs `entitlement_registry` vs `entitlement_license_registry` schema question is NOT decided by this ADR.** `Master_Technical_Architecture.md` defines two separate canonical tables (`license_registry`, per-Membership; `entitlement_registry`, per-Organization); `TDS-C023 §6.3` sketches a single working-name `entitlement_license_registry`. Reconciling these — one table or two, exact columns, constraints, indexes — is a **separate future Technical Design / schema-reconciliation activity**, subject to `CLAUDE.md §18` / `§19.4` STOP-and-report at the point of actual schema creation, exactly as `TDS-C023 §6.3` item 12 and `§23` already require. This ADR only determines which service that schema, whatever its final shape, is hosted in.
- **`IRA-C023` Decisions 1–6 are not reopened, altered, or reinterpreted by this ADR.** Decision 1 (Commit Authority mechanism), Decision 2 (Minimum Scope Now), Decision 3 (catalog governance — deferred), Decision 4 (Consumption/Allocation authority — deferred), Decision 5 (Minimal Frontend), and Decision 6 (Split Ownership — `membership.license_type` stays C-007-owned, read-only to C-023) all stand exactly as recorded.
- **`TDS-018` and the certified `WP-18` are unchanged.** WP-18 remains CLOSED — CERTIFIED — RELEASE-READY (`CERT-WP-18`, `VV-AUDIT-WP-18`, `RRA-WP-18`, Repository Owner Closure Decision `IMP-REPORT-WP-18 §7`). This ADR consumes WP-18's delivered repository-wide C-003 infrastructure as a dependency; it modifies nothing in it.
- **This ADR does not authorize C-023 / WP-17 implementation.** `IRA-C023` remains 🔴 RED — Not Implementation Ready. `WP-17` remains CHARTERED — IMPLEMENTATION NOT YET AUTHORIZED, NOT YET COMPLETE, NOT CERTIFIED. A separate, explicit Repository Owner Implementation Authorization, followed by the full `CLAUDE.md §19.7b` five-gate closure sequence, remains required before any C-023 implementation begins. This ADR removes one `[D]` blocker from that path; it does not shorten the path.
- A future ADR is required before any C-023 service-extraction or `CommercialService` creation begins; this ADR does not authorize that work.
- **Governance-index impact:** none required. Per this repository's established convention (`DOC-000 §8` "ADR Index" family row; every ADR from ADR-022 onward folded into it without a new `DOC-000` row), a new ADR self-registers by filename in the flat `architecture/07-Decisions/ADR-*.md` sequence and does not add a `DOC-000` row. `DOC-000`'s "ADR-001 through ADR-021" ADR-Index-row text is already stale relative to the current sequence (ADR-035); that pre-existing staleness is disclosed here, not corrected by this ADR.
