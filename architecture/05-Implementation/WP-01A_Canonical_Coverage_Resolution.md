## 1. Executive Summary

WP-01 implements seven Business Activities against five of PE-001-C004's seven canonical ERBs (ERB-C004-01, -04, -05, -06, -07). `CERT-WP-01` already found this and classified it as **Finding A**: a documentation/traceability defect in IRA-001 and IMP-REPORT-WP-01 (the "realizing all seven canonical ERBs" claim is inaccurate), explicitly **not** a functional defect, and recommended documentation correction only — "no remediation of `organization_service.py` is implied or recommended by this finding alone."

Independent re-derivation from the canonical specification's own SHALL-level text reaches a **different conclusion than CERT-WP-01's characterization of severity**, while agreeing with its facts. PE-001-C004 v1.1's Organization Lifecycle Contract (§5.4) states unconditionally: *"Once activated, an Organization SHALL exist in exactly one of ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, or RETIRED state... Prior to activation, no Organization exists in this sense; the establishment attempt is held only by its Organization Anchor Context, which SHALL resolve as NOT_FOUND if queried through EX-C004-05."* Business Rule BR-C004-01 states: *"An Organization SHALL NOT be treated as valid before governed activation (ERB-C004-03)."* `OrganizationService.establish()` inserts a row with `status=OrganizationStatus.ACTIVE.value` in the same call that captures identity, with no intervening non-authoritative state, no domain concept, and — critically — **no distinguishable activation act of any kind**: no separate audit action code, no separate domain event, no decision point in the code at all. This is not a case of the canonical "governed no-domain activation" path being exercised (which the specification explicitly permits); it is the complete absence of any activation step, decision, or trace. That distinction is the fulcrum of this resolution (§3, Central Question).

Because what ERB-C004-01 is specified to produce (a non-authoritative Organization Anchor Context / Candidate Organization Identity Context) is never produced — what is produced instead is directly ERB-C004-03's specified output (the first Authoritative Organization Context, in ACTIVE state) — **ERB-C004-01 is implicated, not just ERB-C004-02/03.** This independently confirms the reported disposition-review conclusion, arrived at here from the primary canonical text rather than accepted on report.

This is a genuine, non-trivial gap against explicit canonical Business Rules (BR-C004-01, Contract 5.4), not merely an undisclosed scope reduction. Closing it correctly requires revising already-shipped, already-certified BA-01 behavior (and, depending on design choice, touching BA-02/BA-03's read paths), not merely adding new endpoints. That scope profile — revising accepted Business Activities, not just adding new ones — is why this resolution recommends **OPTION C** (§7), not Option A or a narrower Option B.

Two things are equally important to state plainly, in the spirit of genuine openness this task required:
- **The gap is not that WP-01 omitted domain verification.** PE-001-C004 itself, via its v1.1 "governed no-domain activation" mechanism, explicitly licenses omitting domain verification. That much of the current design is canonically defensible.
- **The gap is that establishment and activation were never built as two distinct, governed, separately-traceable acts at all** — not even in the no-domain case the specification anticipates. Nothing in `PE-001-C004`'s no-domain language authorizes collapsing the *distinctness* of establishment and activation, only the *domain-verification precondition* to activation.

## 3. Current Implementation Mapping

### 3.1 Central Question — resolved first, since it governs all three ERB analyses below

**Does PE-001-C004's "governed no-domain activation" mechanism license the entire current implementation, or only the omission of domain verification specifically?**

Every occurrence of "no-domain" in the canonical text (quoted with surrounding context):

1. **§1.2 Revision History (v1.1):** *"Makes governed no-domain activation semantics unambiguous: a governed no-domain activation path waives only the requirement for a verified primary domain at activation, never domain verification itself, and never converts an unverified or failed domain claim into a verified or trusted one."*
2. **ERB-C004-02, Quality Expectations:** *"...a governed no-domain activation path (ERB-C004-03) never causes an unverified or failed domain claim to be treated as verified."*
3. **ERB-C004-03, Purpose and Business Intent:** *"...together with either a verified Domain Claim Context or a governed no-domain activation decision... A governed no-domain activation decision never verifies, and never causes any capability to trust, an unverified or failed domain claim; it only waives the requirement to have a verified primary domain at activation."*
4. **ERB-C004-03, Entry Context:** *"...together with either a verified Domain Claim Context or a governed no-domain activation decision (never an unverified or failed domain claim)."*
5. **ERB-C004-03, Quality Expectations:** *"A governed no-domain activation path never verifies a domain and never marks an unverified or failed domain claim as trusted; an unverified claimed domain carried into a no-domain activation remains explicitly non-authoritative and non-trusted for SSO or any other trust-dependent behavior."*
6. **EX-C004-04, Trigger / Required Context / AI Assistance / Success Criteria:** each restates the same waiver — verified domain OR a governed no-domain activation decision, "never an unverified or failed domain claim" — and AI "SHALL NOT... represent an unverified or failed domain claim as verified or trusted under a governed no-domain activation decision."
7. **Contract 5.2 (Domain Claim Verification Contract):** *"A governed no-domain activation path (Contract 5.4) waives only the requirement to have a verified primary domain at activation; it SHALL NOT waive, satisfy, or substitute for domain verification itself."*
8. **§6.3 Exception & Recovery Semantics:** *"An unverified or failed domain claim is proposed to be trusted through a governed no-domain activation decision → Rejected; a governed no-domain activation decision only waives the requirement to have a verified domain at activation, and never converts an unverified or failed claim into a verified or trusted one."*
9. **BR-C004-09:** *"A governed no-domain activation decision SHALL NOT convert an unverified or failed domain claim into a verified or trusted domain."*
10. **VR-C004-09 / §8.2 / §9.4 (Invariants):** identical restatement — the invariant is scoped exclusively to domain trust, never to activation's distinctness as an act.
11. **§9.6 Experience Decision Record:** *"Corrective-pass decision (v1.1): replace ambiguous 'waiver' terminology with 'governed no-domain activation path/decision' throughout, and state explicitly that it waives only the requirement for a verified domain at activation, never verification itself... The corrected terminology... [closes] that reading without changing which activation paths this specification permits."*

**Every single occurrence, without exception, scopes "no-domain" to one thing: whether a verified primary domain is required before activation.** None of the eleven occurrences says anything about whether establishment and activation may be the same act, the same request, or indistinguishable in the audit/event trail. This is the textual basis for answering the central question: **the mechanism licenses only the omission of domain verification. It does not license collapsing establishment and activation into one, undifferentiated step.**

This is reinforced independently by three other clauses that are *not* about domain at all:

- **Contract 5.4 (Organization Lifecycle Contract), unconditional, no domain-path carve-out:** *"Once activated, an Organization SHALL exist in exactly one of ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, or RETIRED state at any point in time. Prior to activation, no Organization exists in this sense; the establishment attempt is held only by its Organization Anchor Context, which SHALL resolve as NOT_FOUND if queried through EX-C004-05 and SHALL NOT be returned as ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, or RETIRED."*
- **BR-C004-01, unconditional:** *"An Organization SHALL NOT be treated as valid before governed activation (ERB-C004-03)."*
- **§9.6 Experience Decision Record, the v1.1 corrective pass that specifically closed off a collapsed reading:** *"Corrective-pass decision (v1.1): replace 'PROVISIONAL (pre-activation)' as a stated Organization lifecycle state with an explicit statement that no Organization exists, in the ACTIVE/SUSPENDED/RETIRED sense, before activation. Rationale: exposing a fourth lifecycle state to dependent capabilities would have invented a state EX-C004-05 could return, contradicting the requirement that a pre-activation Organization Anchor Context resolve as NOT_FOUND rather than as a new lifecycle value."* The drafters considered and rejected inventing a visible pre-activation state — they did not consider, and nowhere authorize, skipping the pre-activation state's *existence as a distinct experience* altogether.

**Who makes the "governed" decision, and is it distinguishable from record-creation itself?** Participating Personas for ERB-C004-03/EX-C004-04 are explicitly human/administrative: *"Platform Steward; Organization Steward"* (never "System-facing," unlike ERB-C004-04's Resolve, which is explicitly *"System-facing; invoked by a dependent capability's own Enterprise Experience"*). EX-C004-04's AI Assistance clause: *"AI MAY confirm completeness of preconditions. It SHALL NOT activate an Organization on its own authority..."* — confirming activation is understood as a deliberate human/administrative act, not a system-automatic consequence of establishment. Navigation Expectations for EX-C004-04: *"Navigation SHALL make activation an explicit, named, non-reversible-to-candidate action."* Contract 5.7 (Navigation Contract): *"Establishment, verification, activation, identity change, suspension, reactivation, and retirement SHALL each be explicit, named navigation actions... never an incidental side effect of any other capability's experience."*

**Steelmanning Option A one step further before rejecting it:** could establishment and a no-domain activation decision be the *same* atomic administrative submission, made by the same Organization/Platform Steward, and still satisfy the specification because the two "stages" have zero temporal separation? Even under this most charitable reading, the specification still requires the activation act to be *nameable and traceable as its own thing* — EX-C004-04 must be "immediately resolvable," produce "the first Authoritative Organization Context... handed to every dependent capability as a now-resolvable precondition" (implying a prior state that was not yet resolvable), and Contract 5.7 requires it be an "explicit, named" action, not "an incidental side effect of any other capability's experience." The current implementation produces **exactly one** audit action (`ESTABLISH_ORGANIZATION`) and **exactly one** domain event (`ORGANIZATION_ESTABLISHED`) per `establish()` call — there is no second, distinguishable trace of an activation decision having been made, no field recording *why* no domain was claimed or that a no-domain path was chosen, and no conditional logic in the code representing a decision point at all. `establish()` is unconditional: it always writes `status=ACTIVE`. This is not the licensed no-domain path being exercised — it is the complete absence of an activation experience, decision, or governance act. **Option A therefore fails even under its most generous reading.**

### 3.2 ERB-C004-01 — Establish Organization Identity

1. **Canonical Business Purpose:** *"Enable a new Organization establishment attempt to begin under a stable, explicitly non-authoritative Organization Anchor Context, and capture the minimum candidate identity the Enterprise Operating System requires, before any dependent capability can operate against it."*
2. **Entry Context:** *"An enterprise objective requiring a new Organization, with a Person precondition (C-006) for the requester."*
3. **Exit Context:** *"An Organization Anchor Context holding a Candidate Organization Identity Context sufficient for domain verification and activation."*
4. **Mandatory Business Rules:** BR-C004-08 — *"An Organization Anchor Context SHALL NOT be treated as, or represented as, an Authoritative Organization Context or Organization Validity Context under any circumstance."* VR-C004-07 — the Anchor "is never returned as Organization Validity Context and never treated as Authoritative Organization Context." Quality Expectations: *"the Organization Anchor Context is never presented as proof the Organization exists or is valid."*
5. **Mandatory Data Requirements:** Created/Produced: "Organization Anchor Context for this establishment attempt; Candidate Organization Identity Context held by that anchor... Preserved: Nothing authoritative yet." The current schema (`models/organization.py`) has **no** representation of an Anchor or Candidate state distinct from the final authoritative row — every column exists only on the one `organizations` table, and a row's mere existence *is* its authoritative record.
6. **Mandatory User Experience:** Navigation Expectations (EX-C004-01/02) require establishment to be discoverable and explicit, "never an incidental side effect." Nothing canonically requires a *human confirmation step separate from data capture* for establishment itself — establishment's own Exit Context is reached by data capture; the confirmation requirement attaches to **activation** (ERB-03), not establishment.
7. **Mandatory Domain Objects:** Organization Anchor Context — **not constructed**, implicitly or otherwise; the first persisted artifact is already the final row. Candidate Organization Identity Context — at best transiently implicit as the incoming Pydantic request DTO (`EstablishOrganizationRequest`) for the duration of the function call, never persisted as a distinct non-authoritative construct. Domain Claim Context — absent (see ERB-02). Authoritative Organization Context — **this is what's actually constructed**, from the first moment, which is architecturally ERB-03's output, not ERB-01's.
8. **Current WP-01 implementation coverage:** `OrganizationService.establish()` (`Backend/Services/AuthService/services/organization_service.py:48-117`) performs a duplicate `organization_code` check, then `self.organization_repo.create({..., "status": OrganizationStatus.ACTIVE.value})` in one step, flushes, records one audit entry (`ESTABLISH_ORGANIZATION`), and publishes one event (`ORGANIZATION_ESTABLISHED`) carrying `"status": organization.status` — already `ACTIVE`. `models/organization.py` has no column, table, or enum value representing a non-authoritative state. `GET /organizations/{id}` (BA-02) and `GET /organizations` (BA-03) will return this record, with `status: "ACTIVE"`, immediately and unconditionally after `establish()` returns.
9. **Coverage Classification: Partially Implemented.** The identity-capture substance (name, code, type, description, duplicate-code detection) is genuinely present and reusable — it is not simply missing. What is missing is the entire non-authoritative character of this ERB's own Exit Context: the thing produced is not "an Organization Anchor Context holding a Candidate Organization Identity Context," it is directly an Authoritative Organization Context in ACTIVE state, which is a different ERB's specified output. This is not a documented, governed simplification anywhere in ADR-003/004/005 — those ADRs were decided 2026-07-20, one day before `PE-001-C004` was read for the first time (IRA-001 §15's own account), so they cannot constitute an informed, canonical basis for this specific collapse.
10. **Minimum additional implementation to close to Fully Implemented:** `establish()` must stop producing a persisted, authoritative, ACTIVE row directly. It must produce a genuinely non-authoritative representation — either (a) a dedicated establishment-attempt/anchor table distinct from `organizations`, populated with the candidate identity fields and no lifecycle status at all, or (b) if kept on the same table, a status value that is structurally invisible to every existence/validity/search read path (BA-02, BA-03, and any future EX-C004-05 resolver) rather than merely an additional value in the same enumerable set the Lifecycle Contract already closed off (§9.6 rejected exposing a fourth *lifecycle* value — a pre-activation representation that is invisible to lifecycle resolution entirely, rather than a fourth lifecycle state, is consistent with that rejection). Option (a) is the cleaner, lower-risk design (see §5).

### 3.3 ERB-C004-02 — Verify Organization Domain Claim

1. **Canonical Business Purpose:** *"Confirm that a claimed primary domain is legitimately controlled by the Organization asserting it, before that claim is trusted by downstream identity or access provisioning (C-001/URA-001 SSO domain-based flows)."*
2. **Entry Context:** *"The Organization Anchor Context holding a Candidate Organization Identity Context with an asserted primary domain, or, for a stewardship-initiated domain change, the active Organization's Identity Change Context."*
3. **Exit Context:** *"Domain Claim Context marked verified or unverified/rejected, correlated to the same Organization Anchor Context (or, for a stewardship-initiated change, the active Organization), which remains preserved regardless of the verification outcome."*
4. **Mandatory Business Rules:** BR-C004-02 — *"A domain claim SHALL NOT be trusted downstream before independent verification."* Contract 5.2 (quoted in full at §3.1). BR-C004-09 (no-domain never converts unverified→trusted).
5. **Mandatory Data Requirements:** Created/Produced: "Domain Claim Context, correlated to the same Organization Anchor Context." No such construct, column, or table exists anywhere in the current schema. There is no `primary_domain` field on `Organization` at all — not unset, not nullable-and-unused: **absent**.
6. **Mandatory User Experience:** Navigation Expectations for EX-C004-03: *"Navigation SHALL retain the pending claim. Returning SHALL restore the last valid verification state."* This presumes a genuine, potentially asynchronous verification interaction (proof-of-control submission) — canonically appropriate for a human-facing step, though the specification does not forbid an instantaneous no-domain decision from being system-mediated within a single administrative flow, provided it is still a recorded decision (§3.1).
7. **Mandatory Domain Objects:** Domain Claim Context — **entirely absent**, in every sense: no field, no verified/unverified/no-claim tri-state, no audit metadata distinguishing "domain not claimed" from "domain claim silently ignored."
8. **Current WP-01 implementation coverage:** No code anywhere in `models/organization.py`, `schemas/organization.py`, `services/organization_service.py`, or `routers/organization.py` references a domain concept. `EstablishOrganizationRequest` has no domain field to omit — the concept was never modeled, not merely left empty.
9. **Coverage Classification: Missing** (not Deferred). PE-001-C004's own no-domain mechanism *could* provide a canonical basis to defer real domain verification (a genuine "Deferred" classification requires a canonical or governance basis for the deferral — here, one exists in principle). But "Deferred" also requires that the licensed alternative actually be exercised as a recorded decision. It is not: there is no `no_domain_activation_reason`, no flag, no audit metadata anywhere indicating that a no-domain path was deliberately chosen for any given organization. The omission is unconditional and undifferentiated from an organization that never had the concept considered at all — which is precisely what CERT-WP-01 §4.2 found ("this gap is not disclosed anywhere as a deliberate scope decision"). Absent a recorded decision, this is Missing, not Deferred.
10. **Minimum additional implementation to close to Fully Implemented (governed no-domain path, not full domain verification infrastructure):** Add a nullable `primary_domain` column and a `domain_verification_status` field (e.g., `NOT_CLAIMED` / `PENDING` / `VERIFIED` / `REJECTED`) to the Anchor construct introduced for ERB-01 (§3.2, point 10). Add a `no_domain_activation_reason` (nullable text) captured at activation time when no domain is claimed or verification did not succeed, satisfying BR-C004-09/Contract 5.2's requirement that the no-domain path be a *decision*, not a silent default. Real proof-of-control verification (DNS/email token, etc.) is **not** canonically required for Fully Implemented status if the no-domain path is what a given establishment exercises — that is the entire point of the mechanism analyzed in §3.1.

### 3.4 ERB-C004-03 — Activate Organization

1. **Canonical Business Purpose:** *"Transition an Organization establishment attempt — held by its Organization Anchor Context, with complete Candidate Organization Identity Context and either a verified Domain Claim Context or a governed no-domain activation decision — into an active tenant boundary other capabilities may depend upon."*
2. **Entry Context:** *"The Organization Anchor Context for the establishment attempt, holding a complete Candidate Organization Identity Context, together with either a verified Domain Claim Context or a governed no-domain activation decision (never an unverified or failed domain claim)."*
3. **Exit Context:** *"The first Authoritative Organization Context for that Organization Anchor Context, in ACTIVE state."*
4. **Mandatory Business Rules:** BR-C004-01 (quoted in full, §3.1). Contract 5.1: *"There SHALL NOT be more than one current Authoritative Organization Context for the same Organization Anchor Context."* Contract 5.4 (quoted in full, §3.1). Quality Expectations: *"Activation is irreversible as an event (an Organization is never 'un-activated' back to candidate state; only suspended or retired going forward), deterministic, and immediately resolvable by ERB-C004-04."*
5. **Mandatory Data Requirements:** Produced: "The first Authoritative Organization Context for that Organization Anchor Context, in ACTIVE state, with an effective date and its canonical identity reference now authoritative, handed to every dependent capability as a now-resolvable precondition." Created: *"Nothing structurally new beyond what establishment already captured — activation transitions the existing anchor and candidate context rather than creating new identity content, the defining difference from ERB-C004-01."* This is precise and important: activation is defined as a **transition of pre-existing anchor/candidate data**, which requires that anchor/candidate data to exist first and separately — which it currently does not (§3.2).
6. **Mandatory User Experience:** Navigation Expectations: *"Navigation SHALL make activation an explicit, named, non-reversible-to-candidate action."* Participating Personas: Platform Steward, Organization Steward (human/administrative — §3.1). AI Assistance: *"AI SHALL NOT activate an Organization on its own authority."* This is a genuine, mandatory confirmation act distinguishable from record-creation, per the analysis in §3.1.
7. **Mandatory Domain Objects:** Authoritative Organization Context, "the first" for a given Anchor — this is the only construct actually produced by the current code, but it is produced by `establish()`, not by any distinct activation step. There is no second method, endpoint, audit action, or domain event representing this ERB's own transition.
8. **Current WP-01 implementation coverage:** `OrganizationService.activate()` (`services/organization_service.py:200-295`) exists, but — confirmed by direct reading — it operates only on organizations already in the system: it 404s if not found, 409s if `status == RETIRED` or `status == ACTIVE`, and only proceeds when `status == SUSPENDED`, transitioning `SUSPENDED → ACTIVE`. Its own docstring identifies it correctly: *"Business Activity: Activate Organization (BA-05)... Business Rule: only a SUSPENDED organization may be activated."* This is ERB-C004-06/EX-C004-09 (Reactivate Suspended Organization), exactly as IRA-001 §15 already renamed it to disambiguate. There is **no code path anywhere connecting `establish()` to `activate()`** — a freshly established organization is created with `status=ACTIVE` directly and therefore can never legitimately reach `activate()` (which 409s on an already-ACTIVE organization). `routers/organization.py` exposes exactly one write endpoint for creation (`POST ""` under the organizations router, i.e. `POST /organizations`) and no separate `confirm-establishment`/`verify-domain`/`activate-candidate` endpoint of any kind — confirmed by enumerating all `@router.post`/`put` declarations (`POST /organizations`, `PUT /{id}`, `POST /{id}/activate`, `POST /{id}/suspend`, `POST /{id}/retire`).
9. **Coverage Classification: Missing.** There is no ADR, ERB, or governance record anywhere authorizing the omission of a first-time activation step; ADR-004's "Organization Lifecycle (create, activate, suspend)" line (dated 2026-07-20, one day before `PE-001-C004` was ever read per IRA-001 §15) cannot serve as an informed deferral of a canonical requirement its authors did not yet know existed (see §3.5 below for the full ADR-004 analysis).
10. **Minimum additional implementation to close to Fully Implemented:** A distinct Business Activity/endpoint (e.g. `POST /organization-establishment-attempts/{id}/activate`, kept lexically and semantically distinct from the existing `POST /organizations/{id}/activate`, which must continue to mean only reactivation per BA-05/ERB-C004-06) that: (a) requires the Anchor's domain-verification field to be `VERIFIED` or requires an explicit `no_domain_activation_reason` to be supplied; (b) is restricted to the Platform Steward/Organization Steward personas; (c) creates the actual `organizations` row (or flips a previously-invisible row to visible) only at this point, with its own audit action code (e.g. `ACTIVATE_ORGANIZATION_ESTABLISHMENT`) and its own domain event, kept distinct from BA-05's `ORGANIZATION_ACTIVATED` (Created must stay distinct from Produced, and this ERB's Produced context must stay distinct from BA-05's, exactly as the specification's own "seven-dimension context engineering, Created kept distinct from Produced" discipline requires throughout).

### 3.5 Supplementary finding — what "activate" meant in ADR-004 when it was written

ADR-004 (decided 2026-07-20) lists WP-01's approved scope as including *"Organization Lifecycle (create, activate, suspend)."* ADR-005 (same date) frames the same scope as *"activation/suspension,"* explicitly stating: *"WP-01's approved scope includes 'Organization Lifecycle' (activation/suspension), which has no canonical state model defined anywhere — only a boolean `is_active`... / `active_flag`,"* and its Decision names only a two-value interim model, *"e.g., `ACTIVE`/`SUSPENDED`."* Neither ADR mentions a domain concept, an Anchor Context, a Candidate Identity Context, or any notion of establishment being distinct from activation.

IRA-001 §15 (WP-01 Scope Reconciliation, dated 2026-07-21 — the day *after* both ADRs) states plainly: the `PE-001-C004` docx *"had never actually been read"* before that date; it was *"flagged in this IRA's header as unreviewed, and in Risk #4 (§8) as a Major, Open risk."* §15 itself independently discovered and corrected a related naming collision — renaming the WP-01 Business Activity originally called "Activate Organization" to **"Reactivate Suspended Organization"** specifically *"to remove a premature-authority implication"* and to distinguish it from ERB-C004-03's different "Activate Organization."

**Conclusion:** at the time ADR-004/005 were written, "activate" meant nothing more precise than "the state a newly-created organization holds, as one value of a simple boolean/enum toggle" — it was not, and could not have been, a reference to ERB-C004-03's distinct, governed, first-time-activation transition, because the authors had not yet read the document that defines that transition. ADR-004/005 therefore provide **no canonical or governance basis** for deliberately deferring ERB-C004-02/03 — they simply predate the discovery that those ERBs exist as distinct requirements. This is consistent with, and reinforces, CERT-WP-01's own finding that the gap "is not disclosed anywhere as a deliberate scope decision."

### 3.6 IMP-001 and incremental/ERB-by-ERB delivery

IMP-001 contains zero occurrences of "ERB" or "Enterprise Experience Blueprint" anywhere in its ~11,935 lines (confirmed by full-text search) — it has no opinion, permissive or restrictive, on whether a capability's canonical ERBs may be realized incrementally across work packages, and no concept of a Business Activity legitimately merging multiple ERBs under a governed simplification path. That question is entirely a PE-001/PE-001-Cxxx and IRA/governance-process concern, not an IMP-001 concern. IMP-001 §6.2 ("CorpStage is an Activity-Driven Platform... implementation shall be organized around Business Activities rather than CRUD operations") and §6.8 ("avoid technical activities such as Update Row, Save Record, Delete Entry" in favor of business-meaningful ones) speak only to how an individual Business Activity should be named and scoped, not to whether establishment and activation may be merged. If anything, §6.8's own philosophy cuts against the merge: "Establish Organization Identity" and "Activate Organization" are each independently business-meaningful (the canonical specification gives each its own Purpose, Business Intent, and Business Rules) — collapsing them into one undifferentiated administrative action is closer to a *technical* simplification ("insert one row") than to the business-meaningful granularity §6.8 asks for. IMP-001 does not forbid the current design, but it does not support it either; it is silent, and the governing text is PE-001-C004's own, which is not silent (§3.1).

ARCH-000 (full document reviewed) contains no reference to C-004/Organization Management and no general principle on incremental delivery or MVP scoping across work packages beyond its Architectural Principles (§7) — "Constitutional architecture always takes precedence over engineering implementation" (Principle 10) and "Implementation must never redefine architecture" (Principle 4) — which, applied here, support treating PE-001-C004's SHALL-level text as binding rather than negotiable by WP-01's own convenience.

### 3.7 ERG-001-03 dependency check

ERG-001-03 (Node-to-Membership Linkage) states: *"Every EnterpriseNode that can serve as a membership's organizational home must be addressable and resolvable at the time a Membership is created — the ERG exposes a lookup capability returning valid candidate home nodes for a given Organization, and rejects a Membership creation request that references a node outside that organization's graph or a node in a non-ACTIVE lifecycle state."* This is a dependency on **the EnterpriseNode's own lifecycle state**, and on "Organization exists and is valid" as a precondition for the graph to be addressable at all (per PE-001-C004's own Primary Specification Reference, quoted at Document Control: ERG-001-03 "treat[s] Organization as a pre-existing boundary rather than a canonical object ERG-001 itself engineers"). Nothing in ERG-001-03's text creates a hard dependency on domain verification having occurred, or on a distinct first-time-activation step having been separately executed — it only needs a true/false "Organization exists and is valid" fact, which is exactly what EX-C004-05/Organization Validity Context is specified to answer. **This means the ERB-01/02/03 gap is not something ERG-001 or URA-001-17b currently need fixed for their own contracts to function** — the requirement for a distinct establish/activate separation is intrinsic to C-004's own canonical self-consistency (BR-C004-01, Contract 5.4), not derived from an unmet downstream need. This is a genuinely important, non-obvious point for the "Recommended Resolution" (§7): the urgency of this gap is architectural-integrity urgency, not an active production incident affecting a dependent capability today.

## 5. Minimum Required MVP

The smallest concrete change set that achieves full canonical coverage of ERB-C004-01/02/03, consistent with the governed no-domain path being canonically sufficient (i.e., this does **not** require building real DNS/email domain-control verification):

1. **New construct for the pre-activation Anchor** (separate table, e.g. `organization_establishment_attempts`, rather than a new status value on `organizations` — this is the design that cleanly satisfies Contract 5.4's "SHALL resolve as NOT_FOUND" requirement without touching BA-02/BA-03's existing, already-certified query logic at all, since a row that isn't in `organizations` yet is trivially NOT_FOUND to anything that only ever queries `organizations`):
   - Candidate identity fields mirroring `EstablishOrganizationRequest` (`organization_code`, `organization_name`, `organization_type`, `description`).
   - `primary_domain` (nullable).
   - `domain_verification_status` (`NOT_CLAIMED` / `PENDING` / `VERIFIED` / `REJECTED`).
   - `no_domain_activation_reason` (nullable text) — populated only when activation proceeds without a verified domain, making the governed decision itself a recorded fact rather than a silent default.
   - Requester/actor and timestamp fields (mirroring the Person-precondition context already required by ERB-C004-01's Entry Context).
2. **Revise `establish()` (BA-01):** writes only to the new Anchor construct; returns an establishment-attempt identifier and candidate-identity payload, not an `OrganizationResponse`, not a `status`, not an `organizations.id`.
3. **New Business Activity — Verify Organization Domain Claim (ERB-C004-02):** an endpoint (e.g. `POST /organization-establishment-attempts/{id}/verify-domain`) that records a verification outcome against the Anchor's `domain_verification_status`. Optional to invoke — an attempt with no domain claimed may proceed straight to activation via the no-domain path.
4. **New Business Activity — Activate Organization, first-time (ERB-C004-03):** an endpoint lexically and semantically distinct from the existing `POST /organizations/{id}/activate` (e.g. `POST /organization-establishment-attempts/{id}/activate`). Preconditions: Anchor's candidate identity is complete, and either `domain_verification_status == VERIFIED` or `no_domain_activation_reason` is supplied. On success: creates the `organizations` row for the first time, with `status=ACTIVE`; records a distinct audit action and a distinct domain event from both `ESTABLISH_ORGANIZATION`/`ORGANIZATION_ESTABLISHED` and BA-05's `ORGANIZATION_ACTIVATED`.
5. **No change required to `models/organization.py`'s `OrganizationStatus` enum** (still `ACTIVE`/`SUSPENDED`/`RETIRED`) and **no change required to BA-02/BA-03/BA-04/BA-05/BA-06/BA-07** under this separate-table design, since a pre-activation Anchor is structurally invisible to every query that only ever touches `organizations` — this is the reason the separate-table design is recommended over adding a new status value to the existing table (which the specification's own v1.1 corrective pass already rejected doing for lifecycle purposes, §3.1).

No code is proposed or written here; the above is a description of the minimum change set only.

## 7. Recommended Resolution

**OPTION C — WP-01 scope must be restructured.**

Rationale for choosing C over A or B, stated against the genuine-openness instruction this task carried:

- **Against Option A:** CERT-WP-01's own characterization of Finding A as purely "documentation-accuracy... not a functional defect" does not survive direct re-derivation from PE-001-C004's own SHALL-level text. BR-C004-01 and Contract 5.4 are unconditional, textually explicit Business Rules, and `establish()` violates them as built — every organization is "treated as valid" (immediately ACTIVE, immediately resolvable, immediately usable by ERG-001-03/URA-001-17b) with zero governed activation act ever having occurred, traced, or recorded. Section 3.1 shows this is true even under the most charitable reading of the "governed no-domain activation" language, which is textually and by its own v1.1 corrective-pass history scoped to domain trust alone, never to establishment/activation distinctness. A documentation-only correction would accurately describe the gap but would not close a live non-conformance against two explicit Business Rules.
- **Against a narrower Option B (add new Business Activities only, leave BA-01/02/03 untouched):** the minimum viable fix (§5) requires `establish()` itself (BA-01) to stop producing an authoritative, ACTIVE record directly — this is a revision to an already-shipped, already-reviewed, already-committed Business Activity's response contract and behavior, not an additive Business Activity bolted alongside it. CLAUDE.md §19.7's Business Activity Completion Gate treats a committed BA as complete; reopening BA-01's behavior is a scope-restructuring act, not a new-BA addition, which is precisely what distinguishes Option C from Option B in this determination.
- **For Option C specifically:** the revised roadmap below is additive in almost every other respect (BA-02 through BA-07's already-certified logic is unaffected under the recommended separate-Anchor-table design, §5/§6) — this is a targeted restructuring of the establishment/activation boundary only, not a wholesale WP-01 rework.
