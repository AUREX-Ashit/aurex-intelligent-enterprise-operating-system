**Type:** Implementation planning artifact (process document, not a canonical specification) — companion to `METH-003_Implementation_Methodology_v2.md`
**Established by:** Repository Owner Instruction "Implementation Methodology v2.0 Establishment / WP-10 Planning Authorization," 2026-08-02
**Remediated:** 2026-08-02, per "Final Validation & Remediation before WP-10 Implementation," correcting four misclassifications independently found by `INDEPENDENT-VALIDATION-SER001-HISTORICAL-SCREENS.md` (`F1`, `I1`, `G2`, `G3` — each independently re-verified against primary source directly before correction, not corrected on the validation report's own word alone), plus a closing-arithmetic error and an undercounted placeholder-route inventory.
**Method:** Every historical UI concept under `design/historical-concepts/` classified against **business intent, Enterprise Experience contribution, Executive Experience contribution, workflow contribution, UX innovation, and current architectural relevance** — not against visual design alone. **The classification applies to the underlying CONCEPT the screen represents, not necessarily to the historical HTML implementation itself** — a concept may be preserved (KEEP/EVOLVE/MERGE) even where its specific 2026-06-era visual design is not, and no valuable Enterprise Experience or UX innovation is retired merely because its original screen design has become obsolete. Classified against `PE-001`, `SD-001`, `DS-001`, `CAP-001`, `SER-001`, and `WP-REG-001`/`WPR-001`'s own current, verified state — no mapping invented where none exists, per `CLAUDE.md §17`/§19.

## Governing Finding

All 13 historical concept files/screens are branded **"CorpStage 360"** — an earlier product identity — dated 2026-06-01 through 2026-06-21, predating AUREX's own governed implementation entirely. `SD-001` v2.0's own changelog additionally records a document-wide purge of ESG/sustainability/carbon/compliance-product framing ("Never ESG," Law L2) — directly dispositive for the earliest concepts (`01`–`04`), which carry exactly that framing in their own visible copy. **This purge governs surface framing, not underlying business intent** — the remediation below found that several later concepts (`F1`, `G2`, `I1`) either already anticipate and correctly work around L2 in their own logic, or represent a business intent (executive judgment support, evidence-cited discovery) that is independent of the specific ESG-adjacent source names shown, and whose concept survives even where a literal resurrection would not.

**No item receives KEEP CONCEPT as a wholesale resurrection of its original screen.** Seven items now receive EVOLVE CONCEPT (the underlying business intent is real, evidenced, and either already certified or traceable to already-locked canonical architecture, even though no chartered Work Package yet exists for several of them) — an increase from three, following this remediation. One item receives MERGE CONCEPT. Six receive RETIRE CONCEPT — the underlying idea itself, not merely its 2026-06 screen design, has no surviving business case (self-service commercial signup against an undefined commercial model; a manually-operated internal sales console the platform's own commercial architecture doesn't work the way this screen assumes).

## 3. Pattern-Level Note — `CorpStage Intelligence Center.docx`

Unlike every other historical concept, this document's own underlying structural idea — twelve executive-cognition screens synthesizing Layer-1 evidence into executive judgment support — has an actual, named, canonical counterpart already frozen into `SD-001 §8` (`SD-001-053` through `057`, "Layer 2 Executive Cognition — the Sacred 12"). The specific 12 screen *titles* this `.docx` names (Executive Foresight, Organizational Memory, Cost of Waiting, etc.) appear nowhere in `SD-001`, which defines the Sacred 12 as a **structural/behavioral pattern** (consume-don't-discover, seven-action cap, one-question-per-screen, calm executive tone) — never as 12 named screens.

**This item's own previously-outstanding follow-up is now closed:** this matrix's own prior revision stated this pattern was "worth a distinct future note in `EXECUTIVE-COGNITION-REALIZATION-STRATEGY.md`" — that note was never written. Per the Independent Validation's own finding, this gap has been remediated by registering the pattern directly in `SER-001` as **`SE-062`** (Section 10, added 2026-08-02), rather than only as a cross-document note that itself risked being lost a second time. Once a capability exists to populate Sacred-12-conformant executive screens (most plausibly `SER-001 SE-053`/`054`/`055`, extending C-110 KPI Management, or the broader `SE-050` Executive Cognition umbrella — both already named as `SE-062`'s own dependencies), that future Work Package's own ERB should independently re-derive its own screen set from `SD-001 §8`'s own structural pattern directly — never from this `.docx`'s own specific titles.
