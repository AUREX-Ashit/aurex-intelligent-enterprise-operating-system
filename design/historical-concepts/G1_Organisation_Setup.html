2
What financial obligations carry performance conditions?
Financial instruments with performance covenants — bonds, loans, credit facilities, earn-outs — create tracked obligations with measurable financial consequences if missed. CorpStage can find headline debt from filings but cannot find the specific performance conditions attached. These seed the Commitment Integrity Monitor (Q5) and the Financial Resilience P&L (Q3) immediately.
List any bonds, loans, or credit facilities where a financial condition triggers a penalty, rate change, or covenant breach.
Examples: green bonds with milestone covenants, sustainability-linked loans with KPI triggers, revolving credit with leverage ratio conditions, earn-out structures. Each one added here is tracked against actual performance continuously.
3
What has leadership publicly committed to that investors and regulators are tracking?
CEO statements, board resolutions, investor day commitments, and regulatory consent orders create real obligations — but they are rarely machine-readable from public sources. CorpStage found some from filings and transcripts. These are what it could not find. Each commitment entered here is immediately tracked against operational trajectory. The gap between what was said and what is happening is the most valuable intelligence CorpStage generates.

What is your biggest business risk that is not yet in any public document?
This is the question no intelligence platform has ever asked. Executive anxiety signals — the risks leadership sees but has not yet disclosed — are among the most valuable intelligence CorpStage holds. They shape how the Threat Map (Q4) weights signals and which early warnings get elevated before they become formal disclosures. This seeds Living Memory with intelligence that documents will never contain.
What keeps the CEO or CFO awake right now that has not yet appeared in any filing, report, or public statement?
One or two sentences is enough. Write as you would say it — not in formal language. This is confidential to CorpStage and does not appear in any output.

How does your organisation currently collect operational data?
This calibrates the entire intelligence acquisition strategy. A company with manual spreadsheets needs CorpStage to rely on documents and external retrieval — and to ask more questions. A company with integrated systems allows aggressive automatic extraction. Getting this wrong means the platform either over-asks or under-extracts from Day One.
